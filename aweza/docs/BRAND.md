# AWEZA brand book

Owner: brand agent (09). This is the voice and identity guide for the site, packaging, social and ads. `docs/DIRECTION.md` wins on layout and contracts; this file wins on words.

---

## 1. Essence

**AWEZA is the loud friend with good taste.**

We make bold graphic tees in South Africa. The print says the thing out loud. Everything around the print (the site, the photos, the packaging, the captions) stays calm, sharp and expensive. That gap between a serious frame and an unserious shirt is the whole brand.

- **Brand line:** Time of your life.
- **Three words:** Bold. Cheeky. Crafted.
- **One sentence:** Premium cotton for people who say what they meant.
- **What we are not:** a meme page, a novelty shop, a hype brand, a "lifestyle" brand. We never beg, never shout, never explain the joke.

## 2. Audience

Mostly young South African men, late teens to mid 30s. Also women who like the same jokes.

- They shop on their phones, usually on mobile data. Short words, fast pages.
- They have a group chat that roasts each other daily. The shirt is a contribution to that chat.
- They can smell a try-hard brand from a kilometre away. Respect their intelligence.
- They care about the fabric more than they admit. Tell them it is heavy cotton; do not tell them how to feel about it.

Write for the guy who will read the back of the shirt out loud to his friends at the braai, and for the friend who rolls his eyes and then asks where he got it.

## 3. Voice

**Dry. Confident. Self-aware. A wink, never a shout.**

We talk like the sharpest guy at the braai: a bit arrogant about the quality, never about the customer. Write it like a funny text from a mate, then edit it like a copywriter.

### Rules

1. **Short.** UI and headlines: 2 to 9 words. Body: max 3 sentences per block.
2. **One joke per block.** If the headline is funny, the next line is plain and useful.
3. **Second person, present tense.** "You", not "customers". "Ships in", not "will be shipped".
4. **PG-13 on the site.** The prints carry the edge. The copy never repeats adult wording: refer to those prints by product name only.
5. **No real people.** Never name, describe or hint at any real person, including anyone who appears on a print. Describe the design ("a very sweet surprise on the back"), never the face.
6. **No other brands.** Never name another fashion or sportswear brand, a celebrity, a franchise or a character. Payment and fulfilment partners appear only in legal and help copy where clarity needs them.
7. **Punctuation.** Full stops do the work. No em dashes in UI copy. Max one exclamation mark per page (aim for zero). No emoji on the site.
8. **Prices** are always "R649", "R1 500". Never "ZAR", never decimals, never "only".
9. **Buttons are boring on purpose.** "Add to Bag". "Checkout". "Select a size". Clarity beats cleverness on anything you tap.
10. **If a line could appear on any other store, rewrite it.**

### Do and don't

| Don't | Do |
|---|---|
| Elevate your style with our curated collection. | Heavy cotton. Loud prints. Zero apologies. |
| Our must-have tees are perfect for any occasion! | Good for braais, bad for first dates. |
| Unleash your inner boss with this bold design. | A quiet wordmark up front. The job title on the back. |
| Shop now and get FREE shipping on orders over R1500!!! | Free delivery over R1 300. |
| Oops! Something went wrong. Please try again later. | That did not work. Try again in a moment. |
| This tee features a hilarious print of a famous... | A very sweet surprise on the back. |
| Hey there, fashionista! | (Nothing. Just get to the point.) |
| Level up your wardrobe with vibes that slap. | Wear it to the braai. Let them read it. |

### Vocabulary

**Allowed South African words (max one per page, never forced):** Mzansi, lekker, sharp, braai, eish, howzit, now-now, just now, bru (social only), kasi (social only, and only when it is true).

**House words we like:** statement, print, back, cotton, heavy, loud, quiet, drop, bad decisions, no filter, receipts, the lips.

**Banned forever:** elevate, elevate your style, unleash, level up, curated, must-have, game-changer, vibes, wardrobe staple, look no further, fashionista, slay, iconic, obsessed, treat yourself, retail therapy, elevated basics, drip (unless a customer says it first), "for the culture", any emoji on site, any profanity on site.

## 5. Logo

Files in `images/brand/`:

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `logo.svg` | Primary wordmark, ink `#0A0A0A`, outlined serif with ™. Default on white and light tiles. |
| `logo-white.svg` | On ink and dark photos. |
| `logo-red.svg` | Rare: packaging, stickers, a single campaign moment. Never on the site header. |
| `logo-tagline.svg` | Wordmark with *Time of your life.* in the italic. Footer, OG, packaging, end cards. |
| `lips-mark.svg` | Simplified original lips mark for small uses (loader, favicon, separators). |
| `favicon.svg`, `favicon-32.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png`, `icon-192/512.png`, `icon-maskable-512.png` | Browser and home-screen icons. |
| `og.png` | 1200x630 social share. |
| `art/lips-logo.jpg` | THE lips artwork. Hero centrepiece and signature moments. |

**Rules**
- **Clear space:** at least the height of the "A" cap on every side. Nothing enters that box.
- **Minimum size:** 72px wide on screen (the ™ disappears below that; drop it under 96px in custom lockups). 20mm in print.
- **Colours:** ink on white, white on ink, red on white for rare moments. Never red on black for the wordmark, never on a photo busier than a plain wall.
- **The italic** is the wink. Use it only for *Time of your life.*, taglines and one or two words inside a headline. Never set the wordmark itself in italic on the site; the italic logo is a packaging and sticker variant.
- Never stretch, outline, add shadows, gradients or glow, rotate more than 6 degrees, or retype it in another font.
- On site, `AW.ui.logo()` renders the wordmark as live text in DM Serif Display. The SVG files are for social, favicon and print.

## 6. The lips

The lips are the signature: red, open, AWEZA across the teeth. They are our handshake.

- **Show them big.** Hero, signature spotlight, empty bag, 404, footer, page curtain, first-visit intro. Small lips feel like a sticker; big lips feel like a statement.
- **One kiss per screen.** If two red things compete in one viewport, remove one.
- **Never** recolour the lips, put them on a busy photo, add a tongue, drip, sparkle or any extra mouth effect, or pair them with a real person's face.
- On light surfaces, `lips-logo.jpg` uses `mix-blend-mode: multiply` so the white disappears. On dark sections, set it on a white or `--c-tile` panel.
- The simplified `lips-mark.svg` is for small sizes only (under 64px). Above that, use the artwork.

## 7. Colour

Black and white lead (95% of every screen). Greys do structure. **Lip Red `#D7191F` is the only accent and it belongs to the lips.**

Red is allowed only for: the bag count badge, red product badges, the free-delivery bar, the lips glyph, form errors, and one editorial word per page. Red is never a background, never a primary button, never body text.

## 8. Photography

- **Male-first.** Lead with male models in every placement that has a choice. Women appear, but the first image a guy sees should look like him.
- **Crops:** chest and back. The print is the subject. Faces are cropped or secondary; keep `object-position` on the graphic.
- **Light:** soft, even, grey-white studio. Product tiles sit on `--c-tile` with multiply so every shot looks like one set.
- **No** drop shadows, rounded corners, borders or filters on product images.
- **Real-face prints** (`need-a-hug`, `sweet-cupcake`): only on their own product page, in shop and related grids as normal cards, and in the bag. Never in the hero, collection panels, marquee, OG, newsletter, campaign or paid ads. On cards, lead with the side that has no face (`need-a-hug`: back; `sweet-cupcake`: front).
- **Hotdog print:** teaser only ("Next drop"). No price, no Add to Bag until it is a real product.

## 9. Product naming

Names stay recognisable to the print: `[What the print says] Tee`. Keep them short and literal; the joke is already on the shirt.

- Signature Lips Tee · Mini Lips Tee · Sweet Cupcake Tee · CEO of Bad Decisions Tee · Zero Ex's Tee · Captain Save Tee · Need A Hug Tee
- Collections: The Signature Line (*The mouth that runs the place.*) · Bad Decisions Club (*Poor choices. Excellent cotton.*) · No Filter (*Thought it. Printed it. Wore it.*)
- Summaries: 1 to 2 sentences. Where the print sits, what it says (if it is PG), one dry line. Never quote adult wording.
- Badges: max 3 products at once. `Signature`, `Bestseller`, `New`. A badge on everything is a badge on nothing.
