# AWEZA launch marketing plan

Owner: brand agent (09). Audience: young South African men, 18 to 34, mostly on Instagram and TikTok, mostly on phones and mobile data. Voice rules live in `docs/BRAND.md`. If a line breaks one of them, it does not ship.

---

## 1. Positioning

**For** young South African guys who are the funny one in the group chat,
**AWEZA is** the graphic tee brand that says the thing out loud,
**on** heavy white cotton that looks expensive enough to get away with it.
**Unlike** novelty shirts that are funny once and shapeless after three washes, AWEZA is designed like a premium label: clean front, loud back, printed to order.

**In one line:** The front is polite. The back says what you meant.

**Why believe it**
- Heavyweight cotton, printed to order, so nothing sits in a warehouse going stale.
- Designed in South Africa, by people who have stood at the same braais.
- Sizes XS to 5XL. Every body gets the joke.
- Free delivery over R1 500, delivered across the country.

**Price stance:** R549 to R749. Not cheap, not precious. We never discount in the first 30 days; scarcity and first dibs do the work instead of sales.

## 2. Launch campaign: "Time of your life"

The idea: every shirt is a story you will tell later. The campaign treats a tee like a souvenir from a night you will deny. Shot like a quiet fashion editorial (white walls, hard light, tight crops on backs), written like a group chat.

### Big idea 1: "Read my back"
- **Headline:** Say it with your back.
- **Line:** The front is polite. The back says what you meant.
- **Visual:** Back-of-shirt portraits only. Guys facing away from camera: in a queue, at a braai grill, on a taxi rank bench, on a stoep at golden hour. No faces, just the print and the shoulders. Hard flash, white tees glowing.
- **Where it runs:** Launch hero on site, Instagram grid (a 9-post back-only takeover), TikTok/Reels "turn around" reveal, Meta and TikTok paid.
- **Hero shirts:** CEO of Bad Decisions, Zero Ex's, Signature Lips.

### Big idea 2: "Receipts"
- **Headline:** Evidence of a good time.
- **Line:** Bad decisions. Good cotton.
- **Visual:** The shirts photographed as if they were evidence: folded in a clear bag with a typed tag ("Exhibit 01, Saturday, 02:14"), on a white table with a till slip, a braai tong, a taxi fare in coins, a voice-note waveform. Flat, forensic, very dry.
- **Where it runs:** Instagram carousels, Stories, packaging insert card, email, the shop editorial tile.
- **Hero shirts:** Bad Decisions Club.

### Big idea 3: "The lips that started it all"
- **Headline:** Time of your life.
- **Line:** The original mouth on the block.
- **Visual:** The red lips art blown up huge: a billboard-sized print pasted on a plain wall, a guy in the Signature Lips Tee walking past it, the lips on his back matching the wall. Slow, cinematic, one red thing per frame.
- **Where it runs:** OG/share image, site signature spotlight, a 15s brand film for Reels/TikTok, wheat-paste posters near campuses and nightlife strips (with permission), sticker drops in every order.
- **Hero shirts:** Signature Lips, Mini Lips.

## 4. Ten TikTok and Reels concepts

All shot vertical, 7 to 20 seconds, natural light or one hard flash, real places (stoep, taxi rank bench, braai stand, campus steps, corner spaza). No faces from the prints. Text on screen in the AWEZA serif or plain white sans, never more than six words.

1. **The Turnaround**
   - Hook: "Wait for the back." (text on screen, guy facing camera, plain front)
   - Shots: front, mid shot, beat drop, he turns, hard cut to the back print close-up, mates laughing off-camera.
   - Sound: trending amapiano log drum build, cut exactly on the drop.
   - CTA: "Say it with your back. Link in bio."

2. **Read It Out Loud**
   - Hook: "Make your mate read this."
   - Shots: one guy shows his back, his friend reads the print aloud deadpan (CEO OF BAD DECISIONS), long silence, both nod.
   - Sound: no music, just room tone and the read. Dry is the joke.
   - CTA: "Tag the CEO in your group."

3. **Evidence Bag**
   - Hook: "Exhibit 01. Saturday night."
   - Shots: hands in gloves slide a folded Zero Ex's tee from an evidence bag, label close-ups, unfold to reveal the back, stamp "CASE CLOSED".
   - Sound: true-crime drone, typewriter clicks.
   - CTA: "Bad Decisions Club. Now open."

4. **Queue Reactions**
   - Hook: "The person behind you sees this."
   - Shots: POV from behind in a queue (coffee, taxi, campus), stranger's reaction implied by a cut to a smirking cashier or a nod, end on the print.
   - Sound: lo-fi groove, ambient chatter.
   - CTA: "Wear the conversation starter."

5. **Heavy Cotton Test**
   - Hook: "Is it actually heavy cotton?"
   - Shots: drop the tee on a scale, hold it to the light, scrunch it, wash cycle timelapse, it comes out looking new.
   - Sound: ASMR fabric sounds, then a bass hit.
   - CTA: "Loud print. Serious shirt."

6. **The Lips Wall**
   - Hook: "We pasted this on a wall."
   - Shots: timelapse of the giant lips poster going up, guy in the Signature Lips Tee walks past, camera matches the wall and his back.
   - Sound: slow soul sample, vinyl crackle.
   - CTA: "The lips that started it all."

7. **Braai Fit Check**
   - Hook: "Fit check, but make it braai."
   - Shots: tongs, smoke, meat flip, slow pan up to the back print, mates in AWEZA in the background.
   - Sound: gqom or amapiano, sizzling foley.
   - CTA: "Free delivery over R1 500. Squad order."

8. **Printed To Order**
   - Hook: "Your shirt didn't exist yesterday."
   - Shots: blank white tee, print reveal (macro on ink), folding, packing with the lips sticker, courier hand-off, doorbell.
   - Sound: mechanical rhythm turned into a beat.
   - CTA: "Made when you order it."

9. **Size Range Line-up**
   - Hook: "XS to 5XL. Same joke."
   - Shots: line of guys of every build, backs to camera, all in CEO of Bad Decisions, camera dollies along, last one turns and shrugs.
   - Sound: marching-band style hype, or a capella hum.
   - CTA: "Every body gets the joke."

10. **Next Drop Is Cooking**
    - Hook: "Something hot is coming."
    - Shots: grill, smoke, tongs lift something covered by a cloth, quick flash of the hotdog artwork (blurred), smash cut to black "DROP 02".
    - Sound: sizzle into a sharp bass drop.
    - CTA: "Get first dibs. Link in bio."

## 7. Drop mechanics (the hotdog print)

The hotdog artwork is the first "next drop" and the test of the first-dibs machine.

1. **Tease, never show.** Only cropped, blurred or partial views of the hotdog print until drop day. Line: "Next drop. Still cooking." No price, no Add to Bag on site until it is a real product.
2. **First dibs list.** Site teaser and footer newsletter collect emails. WhatsApp broadcast opt-in as a second channel (this audience reads WhatsApp before email).
3. **24-hour head start.** List members get the drop 24 hours early with a private link. Public gets it the next day at the same price. We reward speed, not discounts.
4. **Small batch language.** "Printed in small batches" and "Drop 02 / 2026" labels. Because we print to order, we never fake stock counts. Scarcity comes from timing (first dibs window), not lies.
5. **Drop day:** 18:00 on a Friday (after payday where possible). Reel at 17:45, Stories countdown, email and WhatsApp at 18:00.
6. **After the drop:** the item joins the shop under the right collection, the teaser block switches to the next design, and the cycle repeats every 6 to 8 weeks.

## 8. Content rules (non-negotiable)

- **No real faces from prints in ads or marketing.** Need A Hug and Sweet Cupcake never appear in paid ads, the OG image, posters, the hero, the marquee, newsletters or campaign shoots. They live only on their own product page, shop grids and the bag.
- **PG-13 public copy.** Captions, ads, emails and the site never spell out adult wording. Refer to those prints by product name. Paid ads avoid prints with adult words visible.
- **Never reference other brands, celebrities, politicians or real people,** in captions, sounds, hashtags or visuals. No logos in shots (cars, drinks, other clothes): shoot clean or blur.
- **No borrowed trade dress.** Don't parody another brand's ad, logo, slogan or look.
- **Consent always.** Repost customer and creator content only with written permission. Credit them by handle unless they ask not to be named.
- **Music:** use the platform's licensed library for paid and branded content. No unlicensed tracks.
- **Emoji:** max one per caption, usually none. Never on the site.
- **Hashtags:** max three. House set: #AWEZA #TimeOfYourLife #BadDecisionsClub #NoFilter #MzansiStreetwear #MadeInSA.
- **Claims:** only say what is true. "Heavyweight cotton", "printed to order", "sizes XS to 5XL", "free delivery over R1 500" are all checkable. No fake reviews, fake stock counts or fake countdowns.
- **Local and legal:** pricing in Rand, VAT note where applicable, POPIA-compliant opt-ins for email and WhatsApp (clear consent, easy unsubscribe).
