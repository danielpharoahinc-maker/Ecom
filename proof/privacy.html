Privacy Policy

Version 1 · Last updated 12 November 2026

Short version: we collect only what Proof needs to work, we never sell it, and you can delete everything yourself at any time.

What we collect, and why

  • Email address: to sign you in and contact you about your account.
  • Name or nickname, goals, answers from setup: to run the app.
  • Proof you post (photos, voice notes, notes, times): to show your history and your club. Photos and audio are stored privately; only you and the clubs you join can view them.
  • Club membership: so friends can see each other's progress.
  • Reminder settings and your phone's notification address, time zone: only if you turn reminders on, so we can send them.
  • Payment status (plan, dates, Paystack reference): to know if you have access. We never see your card number.

We do not collect your location, contacts, or anything for advertising. We do not use analytics or tracking tools.

Cookies and storage

Proof uses your browser's storage only for things the app needs to work: keeping you signed in, saving your proof on your phone, and remembering your settings. We use no advertising or tracking cookies, so there is no cookie banner to accept.

Who else handles your data

  • Supabase (database, sign-in, file storage, reminders), servers in Frankfurt, Germany.
  • Netlify (hosts the app's pages).
  • Paystack (payments).
  • Your phone's notification service (Apple or Google) delivers reminders, if you turn them on.

They process data for us to run Proof and may not use it for their own purposes. Your data is therefore processed outside South Africa, with these providers.

Children

Proof is made for young people, including under 18s. If you are under 18, a parent or guardian must agree to these terms and this policy before you use Proof, and we ask you to confirm that when you sign up. We collect no more from under 18s than from anyone else, and we never use it for advertising.

Emails

We only send sign-in codes and account emails you trigger. We send no marketing email. If that ever changes, every one will have an unsubscribe link, and you will be asked first. Reminders and club notifications can be turned off under Account, then Reminders.

Your rights (POPIA)

  • See and fix your data: it is all in the app. Ask us for anything else.
  • Delete it: Account, then Delete my account. This removes your account, proof, photos, voice notes, club memberships, reminders and billing record from our systems straight away. Payment records held by Paystack follow their own rules and the law.
  • Complain: you can contact the Information Regulator of South Africa at inforeg.org.za.

Keeping it safe

Everything is private by default. The database only lets you read your own data and your clubs' proof. Connections are encrypted. No system is perfect: if something goes wrong that affects you, we will tell you.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. If you stop using Proof, you can delete it at any time.
